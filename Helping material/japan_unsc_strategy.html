🔴 Threats — How to Defend Against Each

🇮🇷IranMain Antagonist

Will say: "Hormuz is Iranian sovereign territory. Any UNSC mechanism is Western imperialism. We have the right to close the strait in response to illegal sanctions."

"When the tanker crisis of 2019 erupted — when our own vessel was struck — Japan's Prime Minister still flew to Tehran. Not with ultimatums. With dialogue. Our monitoring proposal covers ALL vessels, ALL flags. If the strait is truly peaceful and lawful, what is there to fear from neutral observation? Japan asks Iran to engage — not as an adversary, but as the partner we have always been."
Legal knockout: Iran signed UNCLOS. Article 38 prohibits closing international straits in peacetime. This is not opinion — it is binding treaty law Iran accepted.
🇷🇺Russian FederationVeto Threat

Will say: "This is another Western attempt to weaponize the UNSC against a sovereign state. Russia will not allow the Council to be used to encircle Iran."

"Japan deliberately chose NOT to join the US-led Operation Sentinel — to preserve exactly this neutrality. Our proposal has zero military component. It is observation and dialogue. We ask Russia: do you oppose transparency in international waters? Or only when it is inconvenient?"
Concession that works: Add a preambular clause calling on ALL nations to refrain from unilateral coercive economic measures — implicit criticism of US sanctions. Russia claims a symbolic win and softens on the veto threat.
🇨🇳ChinaPartial Threat — Persuadable

Will say: "China opposes UNSC mechanisms that could justify interference in regional states' internal affairs. Address US sanctions first."

"China and Japan both import massively through Hormuz — every barrel of oil China receives from the Gulf transits the same 33 kilometers. We are equally vulnerable. Our resolution calls for JCPOA revival — which China co-authored. Help us pass what China helped design. Our monitoring body is UNSC-mandated, not US-led — exactly the multilateral framework China always champions."
Private unmod offer: "Let China co-sponsor the JCPOA revival clause only. China gets credit for the diplomatic section. We get the monitoring clause." Let them take the win — your resolution still passes.

⚔️ Rebuttal Toolkit — Any Attack, Instant Counter

"Japan is just following America's lead"
"Japan deployed its own MSDF mission — NOT under US command, NOT inside Operation Sentinel. Japan maintained diplomatic relations with Iran when Washington imposed maximum pressure. We speak for Japan, not for Washington. Our independence is documented, not claimed."
"Your monitoring proposal is a pretext for military action"
"Japan's constitution — Article 9 — prohibits offensive military action. Japan cannot legally launch or join a military strike. Our proposal is structurally incapable of being a military pretext because Japan cannot be party to one. The monitoring mission is civilian, neutral, and UNSC-mandated. Full stop."
"JCPOA failed — diplomacy doesn't work with Iran"
"The JCPOA didn't fail — it was abandoned. The IAEA certified Iranian compliance for three consecutive years. The framework collapsed when the US unilaterally withdrew in 2018 and Europe failed to deliver promised sanctions relief. We don't condemn diplomacy because one party walked away from the table. We return to the table."
"Iran has sovereignty over the Strait"
"Japan fully respects Iranian sovereignty over its territorial waters. But the Strait of Hormuz is an international strait under UNCLOS Article 37 — to which Iran is a signatory. Transit passage is a right under customary international law. Sovereignty ends where binding treaty obligations begin — for every state in this room, including Japan."
"This resolution serves rich countries, not the Global South"
"The countries that suffer MOST from a Hormuz closure are not Japan or the USA — they are Pakistan, India, Sri Lanka, and East African nations with no strategic reserves and no buffer against price shocks. Our IEA emergency reserve protocol extends to non-IEA nations in crisis. This resolution protects the Global South first and foremost."
"Why should we trust Japan as a mediator?"
"Because Japan has earned it. Our tanker was attacked in 2019 — and our Prime Minister still flew to Tehran. We maintained LNG contracts with Iran during maximum pressure. We deployed ships without joining a military coalition. We have active relationships in Riyadh, Tehran, Washington, and Beijing simultaneously. No other country in this room can say the same. That IS what a mediator looks like."
"Japan is a non-regional actor with no standing here"
"Japan is the world's largest LNG importer and a top-three crude oil importer — virtually all of it through Hormuz. Japan's economic presence in this region exceeds many states on this Council. We are not observers. We are stakeholders. And we have the scars — a struck tanker in June 2019 — to prove it."
"Unilateral sanctions by the US are the root cause — address that first"
"Japan agrees that unilateral coercive measures destabilize diplomacy — that is precisely why our resolution calls for UNSC-authorized multilateral frameworks rather than unilateral actions by any power. Japan itself never supported the maximum pressure campaign. Our resolution is the alternative the international community needs."

🏆 Step-by-Step Win Plan

Phase 1 — Opening Round · GSL + Mod 1

Plant Your Flag, Lock Identity

  • Deliver GSL speech and state Japan's 3 pillars clearly. Frame the debate before anyone else does.
  • First unmod — even 60 seconds: find South Korea and India immediately. One sentence: "We're co-sponsoring, right?"
  • Mod 1: pure LEGAL framing — UNCLOS, international law, transit passage. Uncontroversial enough to get Germany, France, UK, and Gulf states nodding early without triggering Russia/Iran.
  • Do NOT engage Iran or Russia aggressively in Phase 1. Appear reasonable, open, buildable. Let them reveal their positions first.
  • Raise a POI to Iran: "Will the delegate clarify Iran's position on UNCLOS Article 38, to which Iran is a signatory?" — puts them on the defensive immediately.
Phase 2 — Mid Debate · Mod 2 + Unmod 1

Build the Bloc, Draft the Paper

  • Move for unmod after Mod 2. Have a draft working paper outline ready on paper — even rough headings.

  • Private pitch to China: "Co-sponsor the JCPOA revival clause only — you get diplomatic credit, we get the monitoring clause. Split the win." Don't ask China for everything.
  • To Saudi Arabia + UAE: offer the tanker seizure condemnation clause specifically — they sign for that acknowledgment.
  • Private red line to USA: "Drop military authorization, we add Iranian UNCLOS violation language." That's the trade.
Phase 3 — Late Debate · Mod 3 + Unmod 2

Merge Papers, Isolate Opposition

  • If a competing paper exists (likely USA bloc or Russia/Iran bloc), propose a merger. You negotiate from 7+ co-sponsor strength.
  • Make your paper so balanced that vetoing it looks purely obstructionist — JCPOA revival, Iranian sovereignty respected, zero military mandate, IEA emergency protocols included.
  • Mod 3 speech: present your full architecture as a complete solution, not a partial measure. Be the closer. Make delegates feel your resolution is the only adult in the room.
  • If Russia signals veto: offer ONE preambular concession — language on "unilateral coercive measures." Russia takes the symbolic win, veto threat softens.
  • Fire your best one-liners here: "We cannot sanction our way to security. We tried that from 2018 to 2023. The result was more tanker seizures, not fewer."
Endgame · Voting Bloc

Control the Count, Win the Narrative

  • Minimum viable: Japan + S. Korea + India + Germany + France + UK + Qatar + Oman + Kuwait = 9. You start there. Everything else is buffer.
  • China is your biggest diplomatic investment. Spend the most unmod time with China — it unlocks Pakistan too.
  • Never — under any circumstances — let USA push military authorization language into the resolution. That single clause triggers simultaneous China AND Russia vetoes and collapses your entire coalition.
  • If Russia vetoes anyway: 11+ votes on record is a diplomatic masterclass. Russia isolated itself. Japan leads. That's the story, and that's the award.
